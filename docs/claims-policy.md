# Sources, claims and compatibility policy

## What a claim must identify

- The exact harness/provider/authentication surface.
- Source or software version and check date.
- What was documented, inspected or actually exercised.
- The savings denominator and any helper/cache/recovery costs.
- Quality evidence, failures and scope limitations.

Do not say “tested” when only a README was read, “safe” because originals were stored, or “compatible” because one text request returned.

## Market coverage

The [landscape](market-landscape.md) discloses search scope and exclusions. A catalogue is not a census of every repository, and a long source list is not a count of independent experiments.

Prioritize major first-party products, established active ecosystems and methods with useful primary evidence. Experimental work can inform a blueprint without becoming a recommended install. Explicitly requested ambiguous names receive an identity boundary rather than an invented match.

## Savings language

Keep these distinct: bytes, eligible payload tokens, context occupancy, total task tokens, cached-input pricing, subscription allowance, billed money, latency and accepted outcomes.

External studies are attributed to their tasks/models and methodology. Scenario estimates declare assumptions. Neither becomes this repository's measured average. Do not add overlapping percentages or hide unsuccessful runs.

No universal “75% average,” “95% with identical quality,” “every harness,” “never breaks,” market-wide uniqueness or self-assigned perfect score. Explain actual strengths and the conditions under which they matter.

## Dates and contradictions

Record the actual check date, not the date a reader wishes a feature existed. Prefer pinned repository revisions for code/schema claims. Mutable official docs establish what was observed, not a feature's first release in every binary.

When official docs conflict, use the narrow common supported route or leave the route limited/unresolved. Preserve the conflicting sources and tell the reader what must be verified on their account. Do not quietly pick the more convenient claim.
