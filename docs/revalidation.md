# Factual revalidation and scope

Reviewed 2026-09-07 UTC. This is a source-backed revision, not an authenticated deployment or savings benchmark.

## What was covered

The review accounted for all 80 files in the preceding revision, all 19 detailed harness guides, 91 auth/provider records, 53 market decisions, 125 market-source records and the pinned compression inventory. Duplicate prose was grouped into material claim families; static assets, templates, code and historical validation had separate dispositions. This is not a claim of 91 live account trials or an independent re-execution of every cited paper.

The revision adds [broader platform coverage](platform-coverage.md) and a [provider-specific authentication matrix](addons/omniroute-auth.md). The coverage distinguishes native workflow methods, documented configurable interfaces, legacy/transition entries and unverified behavior. Surface variants are not counted as independent products.

## Corrections that matter

- API protocol, upstream authentication, entitlement, billing and provider policy are separate dimensions. OmniRoute supports OAuth as well as API-key connections.
- A cautious initial API-key pilot was our recommendation, not a product limitation or universal ban on OAuth gateways.
- Native CLI-owned sign-in, direct token custody and imported cookies/keys have different rules and behavior. Provider-specific restrictions still apply.
- Retired or unavailable route records must not retain generic instructions to start ordinary inference.
- Continue's final release authentication wording and moved/broken Goose/xAI source links were corrected. Confirmed lifecycle changes were retained rather than reversed because they seemed surprising.
- Claude SDK billing follows the current pause notice; that does not grant blanket third-party credential-intermediation permission.
- Builder credits may be per response or other product unit, not proportional to prompt tokens. Source-specific billing is recorded in the coverage catalogue.

## What remains explicitly unverified

Actual accounts, OAuth entitlement/refresh, every tool/protocol combination, exact current managed-service pricing where unavailable, and complete-work savings still require their own checks. Specific Hermes/OpenClaw Claude credential mechanisms and provider-policy questions remain labeled unresolved rather than certified by a project assertion.

The combined Conservative/Aggressive, delegation and gateway stack has no measured universal saving. Published arithmetic was reconciled with its original denominators and rounding limits; it was not converted into a new stack average.

## Verification boundary

The repository checker covers internal navigation, structured inventories, known private-data patterns, syntax and synthetic accounting. New tests prevent the coverage/auth records from losing identities, source fields or their untested boundary. They do not certify factual truth merely because a URL exists.

Executed counts, clean-package results and the observed GitHub account-level CI restriction are recorded in [the validation record](../validation/record.json). Research evidence is retained privately; completed research sessions can be archived without deleting the evidence or the main conversation.
